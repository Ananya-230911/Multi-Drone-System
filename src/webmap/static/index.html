Drone coordination on a map

Simulation only. Pick a place, mark the target; the drones agree among themselves who goes.

1 Find a place

Search uses OpenStreetMap (internet needed). You can also type coordinates like 48.85, 2.35.

2 Operating area

Zoom to where you want it, then click. The rectangle is the area the drones may fly in. The four drones appear inside it: drag them to move them.

3 Target

4 No-fly zone (optional)

5 Drone parameters

Battery %Alt mSensor mFail at s
Sensor reach on the ground = smaller of sensor radius and altitude (45° view). A drone that lacks battery for the round trip cannot be chosen. "Fail at" breaks that drone at the given time to show the others taking over.
